Services

Since 2008, Deb Ellis and her team have staged and designed hundreds of homes across Greater Boston, from Back Bay condominiums to new construction in Wellesley, all of it furnished and installed from our own studio and warehouse in Norwood.

Most of our work comes from three kinds of clients: agents and builders who need a listing ready for photos, homeowners renovating or furnishing a house they plan to live in, and families moving back home after fire or water damage. The same designers and the same install crew handle all three, and every staging job is furnished from our own warehouse.

01Interior DesignWhole-house and single-room design, renovations included. We choose finishes, fixtures and layout with you and your contractor, then furnish and style every room. 02Home StagingWe furnish vacant and occupied listings from our own inventory for agents, builders and homeowners, and we have staged a listing on less than a day's notice when an agent needed it. 03Post-Loss Interior DesignWhen fire, water or storm damage forces a family out, we design and furnish the house for their return. We work from the insurance scope alongside the adjuster and the restoration contractor. 04Custom ProjectsModel homes and units for developers, turnkey staging packages at a set price, holiday installations, and other projects that need our inventory and our install team.

How a project runs

  1. i.

    Walkthrough

    We walk the house with you, measure the rooms, and talk through who the buyer is or how you want to live there. For a listing, we also ask about your photo date and your list date.

  2. ii.

    Proposal

    You get a written plan with the rooms, the pieces and a fixed price before anything moves. Design projects also get a schedule built around your contractor's.

  3. iii.

    Install

    Our team pulls the furniture from Norwood and installs the house in [#] day, with art, rugs, linens and accessories included.

  4. iv.

    Pickup

    When the house sells or the staging term ends, we schedule pickup around your closing date.
