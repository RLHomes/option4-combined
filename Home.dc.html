How a project runs

  1. i.

    Walkthrough

    We walk the house with you, measure the rooms, and talk through the buyer or the way you want to live there.

  2. ii.

    Proposal

    You get a written plan with the rooms, the pieces and a fixed price before anything moves.

  3. iii.

    Install

    Our team pulls the furniture from Norwood and installs the house in [#] day, art and linens included.

  4. iv.

    Pickup

    When the house sells, or the rental term ends, we schedule pickup around your closing date.
